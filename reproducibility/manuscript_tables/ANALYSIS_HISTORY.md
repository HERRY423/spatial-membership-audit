## 2. Explicit Scope, Independence, and Claim Boundaries

We apply uniform evidentiary standards across every dataset:

1. **Public prospective chronology (`nTMA` vs `tTMA1`):** `Wang 2025 tTMA1` remains a retrospective external validation cohort (with `unused_heldout` cores never touched during `r1/r2` development). By contrast, `Wang 2025 nTMA` (15 normal human organs across Xenium Breast, CosMx+IF, MERSCOPE, and Xenium Panhuman) was fetched from Zenodo strictly after public GitHub tag `v3.0.0-protocol-freeze-20261125` (`2026-11-25T14:12:08Z`), evaluated strictly under `DEFAULT_PROTOCOL_V3` (+ optional secondary MITM and fixed baselines) with zero parameter tuning, and reported transparently including extreme sparsity regimes (`Track N-A` CosMx median `3` tx/cell; `Track N-B` MERSCOPE median `2` tx/cell).
2. **Observed-core comparability vs registered physical overlap:** Wang `r1/r2`, `r2/y24`, `tTMA1` Tracks A–C, and `nTMA` Tracks N-A–N-C evaluate serial TMA sections matched by core ID and concordant tissue/organ identity; they test cross-run, cross-panel, and cross-platform comparability on observed core footprints, **not** sub-core registered physical overlap. Physically co-registered single-cell / sub-region accuracy is established by MERFISH+ Heart G3 (same-section reimaging), Spatial Touchstone CosMx Breast (same-cell IF, evaluated both pooled and disaggregated by patient `BR1 4066` vs `BR2 4399`, under both Vendor segmentation and native Linux `Proseg v3.2.0` resegmentation), and M242T HCC (`GSM9815454`, zero-leakage DNA-only registered Xenium + 38-plex dual-segmentation IMC at `0.171 µm` median 16-tile residual).
3. **Donor and spatial clustering:** Because TMA cores within the same TMA block or patient share spatial/donor correlation, `v3.0` reports TMA-block clustered (`12` blocks on `r1/r2` and `tTMA1`, `9` blocks on `r2/y24`), spatial-block clustered (`MERFISH+`, `PR1`, `M242T` 16 tiles), and patient-disaggregated (`BR1 4066` vs `BR2 4399`) metrics alongside core-level intervals. Cross-study generalizability spans **five independent published datasets** (MERFISH+ developing heart, Spatial Touchstone breast/prostate, Wang pan-cancer HTMA/`tTMA1`, Wang normal-organ `nTMA`, and M242T human HCC).
4. **Native Linux competitor parity:** All previously missing Linux-native competitors (`Proseg v3.2.0`, `CRAWDAD 1.0.1`, `spicyR`, `PANORAMIC 0.99.4`) and independent classifiers (`InSituType 2.0`, `CellTypist`) were executed natively on Ubuntu 24.04 Linux with exact package versions archived in `review/v3_linux_competitors_2026-11-25/env_versions.json`. Only `cellAdmix` was not additionally re-run because direct segmentation independence was already established both by native `Proseg v3.2.0` transcript reassignment on 54 CosMx Breast FOVs and by completely independent 38-plex IMC DNA+membrane watershed segmentation on M242T HCC (`16,772` cells).
5. **Full disclosure of unfavorable and boundary outcomes:** Every negative or boundary readout—including (i) pan-cancer paired-t residual false discoveries under small systematic cross-platform pair offsets (`tTMA1` Track A `73.3% → 46.7%`, whereas within-cancer paired-t drops `28.0% → 2.7%` and disjoint two-center Welch drops `36.3% → 3.0%`), (ii) boundary-clamp Type-I error inflation (`18.3%–41.7%`) in extreme simulation cells (`r_d ≤ 0.25, γ_SNR ≥ 2–3`) when `q_A` clamps at `0.10`, (iii) moderate-tissue discard power loss (`16.3 pp` at 75% discarded under `B=10` vs `33.1 pp` under `B=1`), (iv) `20×` (`L → H`) upward-extrapolation failure on M242T, (v) `Xenium PR1` near-balanced CI spanning zero, (vi) HNSCC Core 64 RNA-vs-IF offset under downward rarefaction, (vii) extreme sparsity boundaries on `Wang 2025 nTMA`, (viii) the 35-core 6-gene negative probe (`−36.3%` CosMx/MERSCOPE) and its mechanistic decomposition, and (ix) `tTMA2` `NO_GO_MATERIALS` exclusion—is reported directly in the Main Text and Tables.
